Take the controls

Mouse — click Mouse or press X, then move to steer.
Wheel — throttle. Right click — hold and drag sideways to roll.
Left drag — yaw / pitch steering. F — fire weapons. E — interact. Middle click — boost.
Camera — V switches cockpit / outside view.
Steer — A/D yaw, W/S pitch, or arrow keys.
Throttle — Shift + W/S, or the slider.
Brake — Space. Boost — B.
Cruise — C. Scan — Q. Fire — F or left click.
Jump map — J. Target — T. Approach assist — R.
Recover / Dock — E when close and slow.
Pause — Escape or P.
Hold left mouse and drag to steer. Hold right mouse and drag sideways to spin around the nose-to-tail axis. Touch dragging steers. Esc releases the mouse and pauses. Shift + wheel adjusts chase-camera distance. Approach assist cancels when you steer. Aim at the target bracket; the bearing readout guides you if it is off-screen.
